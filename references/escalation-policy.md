# Routing evidence

The lead decides from evidence. Architectural uncertainty is a lead decision, not an executor capability failure. Small bounded findings may use Luna; implementation revisions normally resume the same external session. Distinct strategy needs may justify another capable external executor. Core difficulty or exhausted external options may justify lead takeover.

Retain advisory complexity signals: three subsystem boundaries +2, unexplained test failure +2, complex persistent/concurrent/migration state +2, eight files +1, roughly 400 meaningful changed LOC +1, toolchain impact +1. Score >=3 suggests checking executor capability and strategy; it does not trigger a hidden Claude/Luna price router.

Repeated blocking criteria, repeated regressions, invalid results or a blocked strategy justify a routing decision. Current executor cap is two rounds; helper cap two; takeover cap one. A permission denial must be handled as a permission issue, not routed to another model to evade it. No automatic routing is shipped in external M0. A new executor always gets a brief of current tree, prior changes, remaining failures, evidence and the lead's current decisions, not another executor's full transcript.
